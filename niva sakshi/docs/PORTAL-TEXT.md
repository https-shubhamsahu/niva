## Idea title

Niva Sakshi: a sensor station that witnesses India's Flamingo balance test

## Idea description (about 140 words)

In the Khelo India school fitness test (Class 1-3) and the Fit India adult test, the Flamingo balance item asks the tester to "pause the stopwatch each time the subject loses balance" and count those losses over 60 seconds of balancing. Niva Sakshi turns the beam the test is done on into a witness. Load sensors in the beam (finale build) and our working sensor insole on the held foot timestamp each step-off or touch-down and pause the clock. The teacher confirms or dismisses every flag and adds any loss the sensors miss, and each trial keeps both. Until it is zeroed, the station refuses to score. No camera is used, so no video of children is recorded. Between test days the same beam runs one-leg practice in PE periods. Results export as CSV for the term record. Agreement with a trained tester and slow-motion video will be measured before the finale.
