Potential challenges and risks

Strategies for overcoming these challenges

  • Radio dies around bends

    ≈ 24 dB lost at a single bend

    AI drops a SUTRA relay at the bend apex · Long-Range fallback · black-box keeps full data

    designed-in

  • Contactless vitals can mislead

    dust, angle, movement

    Fuse radar + thermal + camera, read while perched, props idle · AI only suggests; a doctor confirms

    designed-in

  • Short drone endurance

    caged drones fly about 10 min

    Roll-first mission: rolling cages showed ~4× range in research; fly only over obstacles

    designed-in

  • Voids tighter than the cage

    500 mm cage vs dense rubble

    Tunnels, galleries, stairwells & voids ≥ 0.6 m · SURANG-mini on the roadmap

    roadmap

  • Relay jams or double-drops

    two pucks fall together

    Two-pin escapement + break-beam exit check · one servo, no motor in the relays

    designed-in

  • False survivor alarms

    hot rock, noise, echoes

    Multi-sense fusion + confidence score · a human confirms with one tap

    designed-in

  • Ignition risk in gassy mines

    motors and battery near CH₄

    CH₄ interlock (0.75 % turn back, 1.25 % land) · gas-free trials · Ex-proof → CIMFR → DGMS

    roadmap
