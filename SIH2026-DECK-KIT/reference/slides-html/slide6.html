Research → our design

every innovation is anchored in published work

  • HyTAQ · ICRA 2013 [1]

    A rolling cage gives 4× range, ~6× operating time vs flying

    → SURANG rolls by default, flies only over obstacles

    Roll-fly

  • Appl. Sci. 2026 · mine tunnels [2]

    ≈ 24 dB lost at a single bend in a mine tunnel

    → a SUTRA relay is dropped at each bend apex

    Relay at every bend

  • CERBERUS · DARPA SubT [3]

    Winning robots dropped Wi-Fi breadcrumbs underground

    → our drop brain adds LiDAR bend prediction

    AI relay-drop brain

  • Sci. Rep. 2025 · coal-mine SLAM [4]

    Dropped tagged targets cancel map drift in long roadways

    → every relay carries an AprilTag + radio ID

    Breadcrumb GPS

  • Espressif ESP32-C3

    Built-in Wi-Fi ranging (1–2 m) + Long-Range mode

    → relays range each other with no extra parts

    ₹0 upgrade per relay

  • Fraunhofer 2023 · NIOSH [5]

    Drones hear cries for help; geophones hear tapping

    → rotors idle while perched for clean audio

    Perch & listen

  • Anal. Chem. 2018 · YOLO 2025 [6][7]

    Thermal AI finds people; sensors smell entrapped victims

    → fused cues, confirmed by a human

    Multi-sense search

  • Bioengineering 2023 · drone rPPG [8]

    Pulse from drone video at 5 m: good enough for triage

    → contactless triage colour; a doctor confirms

    Remote triage

  • Codec2 · Bhashini (MeitY) [9]

    Voice in 3.2 kbps; speech AI in 22 Indian languages

    → doctor ↔ victim voice rides the relays

    Talk-through

  • Flyability price list 2023 [11]

    Elios 3: US$51.6k–74.5k + $5.7k range extender

    → our field unit targets 1/16–1/24 of that

    ≈ ₹3 L field unit
