Rounded white athletic sneaker body with deep teal outlines and teal panels (#007F79), coral accents (#F47B65), a lime tongue/lace detail (#D1EF72), sturdy cream sole. Strong clear silhouette, large readable expression at 100px size, polished editorial vector-like illustration with minimal shading, crisp edges, no 3D, no gradients, no photographic textures. Shoe points slightly right in a three-quarter profile; face visible. Entire character centred with generous transparent padding so hands and feet are not cropped. No text, lettering, brand logos, watermark, secondary characters, background, shadow rectangle or scene. Avoid resembling an existing Duolingo character.
