Changes, 9 Nov 2026 (review C items C1, C9, C12, C17, C18; charger IC swap deferred to Rev C.3):
  - D2 added: Bourns SMAJ5.0A TVS (SMA / DO-214AC), cathode on VBUS after F1, anode to GND,
    as MCP73831 DS20001984H 6.1.1.2 and Fig. 6-1 ask for a hot-pluggable USB input.
    Bourns SMAJ datasheet: VRWM 5.0 V, VBR 6.40-7.00 V at 10 mA, IR 800 uA max at 5.0 V,
    VC 9.2 V at 43.5 A (10/1000 us), 400 W, 1.0 W steady state, body height 2.29 mm max.
    Placed at (157.1, 59.2) between J1, C1 and U1, 5 mm from U1 VDD. 0.8 mm copper from the
    cathode to the VBUS run that feeds U1 VDD (that run widened to 0.8 mm), 0.8 mm from the
    anode to the J1 GND run, plus one GND stitching via to the B.Cu pour. No enclosure wall
    sits over this spot (the well walls end at x 148).
    What it does and does not do: its VBR maximum equals the MCP73831 7.0 V VDD absolute
    maximum, so D2 takes the hot-plug overshoot off but cannot hold VDD under 7.0 V in a large
    surge (9.2 V at 43.5 A). Series-RLC model of a hot plug (cable 0.5-1 uH, 0.25-0.6 ohm):
    the old C1 (about 2.2 uF effective) peaked at 7.76 V; with D2 alone that becomes
    6.57-7.12 V; with the new C1 (5-7 uF effective) the peaks are 5.1-7.0 V before D2 needs
    to conduct. A sustained overvoltage makes D2 conduct until F1 trips.
  - F1 = Bourns MF-NSMF050/24X-2 (1206). I_hold 0.50 A at 23 C, 0.44 A at 40 C, 0.26 A at
    85 C, against about 0.11 A drawn (100 mA charge, LED, comparator). I_trip 1.00 A,
    0.10 s max at 8 A, R 0.15-0.75 ohm (about 80 mV at 0.11 A). The 24 V rating lets it hold
    off a faulty 20 V source after D2 has made it trip; the review's MF-NSMF050-2 has the same
    land and current ratings but is 13.2 V. Bourns land 1.0 x 1.6 mm with a 2.0 mm gap sits
    inside the KiCad 1206 land (1.25 x 1.75 mm, 1.55 mm gap): no copper change.
  - C1, C2 = 10 uF 16 V X5R 0805, Murata GRM21BR61C106KE15L (were 4.7 uF 10 V 0603, below
    the datasheet's 4.7 uF minimum once DC bias is counted). Re-landed to 0805 on the same
    centres; no other part moved. Local copper only: C1's VBUS and GND tracks end on the new
    pads, the STAT via under C1 moved 0.6 mm down, C2's GND entry shortened. Effective
    capacitance at 4-5 V should stay above 4.7 uF (typical 0805 X5R DC-bias curve; not
    measured).
  - NTC window retuned (C9): R6 / R7 / R8 = 46.4k / 31.6k / 19.6k 1 %, Yageo
    RC0603FR-0746K4L / -0731K6L / -0719K6L. R5 stays 22k. Thresholds 0.525 / 0.201 of VBUS
    (were 0.570 / 0.180). The review proposed R7 = 33.2k (3.0-41.8 C); 31.6k gives up 1.2 C
    of window for about 0.6 C more margin at each edge once the LM393 full-temperature limits
    and B3380 NTCs are counted. Charge allowed (pack NTC temperature):
        NTC           nominal          worst case
        10k B3435     3.7 .. 41.2 C    2.2 .. 43.0 C
        10k B3380     3.4 .. 41.4 C    1.8 .. 43.3 C
        10k B3950     6.3 .. 39.0 C    4.9 .. 40.5 C

    input (full temperature range), VBUS 4.6-5.25 V. With the NTC at +-3 % the B3435 window
    is 1.7 .. 43.6 C. The old values gave -1.9 .. 47.1 C on the same basis. VTH_HI at
    VBUS 4.6 V is now 2.41 V, inside the LM393 full-range common-mode limit (VCC - 2 V =
    2.6 V); it was 2.62 V (C11).

Wrong pack NTC: what a room-temperature first test does and does not show. This corrects the
earlier claim that a wrong NTC always shows up on the first test.
  With these values the dock charges in a 25 C room for any NTC from about 5.6 k to 24 k
  (at 25 C), but keeps its window inside the cell's 0-45 C only from about 8.5 k to 11.4 k
  (B3435; 7.3 k to 12.7 k for B3950). So:
    - Shows up (no charge in a 25 C room): 33 k, 47 k and 100 k NTCs, and anything below
      about 5.5 k. Even then a 47 k (B4050) part charges a pack at 40-81 C and a 100 k
      (B4250) part at 58-101 C, and a 4.7 k part still charges in a room below 21 C.
    - Does NOT show up (charges normally at room temperature): 6.8 k (charges down to
      -4.7 C), 12 k B3435 (up to 46.5 C), 15 k B3950 (14.5-49.3 C), 22 k B3950 (22.8-59.7 C).
  So incoming inspection is the check: measure every pack's NTC between the NTC and P- leads
  at 25 C before soldering, accept 10 k +- 3 %, record the lot.

Passing KiCad ERC/DRC is not a design review. Still open:
  - Rev C.3: replace the MCP73831 with a charger that has a charge safety timer (C2; the
    EEMB cell spec asks for one) and a 30 V-tolerant input with OVP (C1), for example TI
    BQ25170 with its TS pin. Until then charge attended only, and stop by 5 h (F4).
  - No hysteresis on the NTC window (C10). The review's 4.7 Mohm from TEMP_OK to VTH_HI was
    not fitted: it gives +0.46 C at the cold edge, but VTH_LO hangs off the same divider, so
    it moves the hot threshold the wrong way (-0.35 C) and TEMP_OK would oscillate in a band
    near 41 C. Hysteresis on both edges needs VTH_LO on its own divider (one more resistor,
    R7 to GND); the C.3 TS-pin charger makes it moot. Cold-edge on/off cycling from the P-
    return drop stays a bench item.
  - No ESD part on J2 NTC_S / BAT; a half-seated cartridge can pull the LM393 inputs below
    -0.3 V (C13).
  - One LED cannot tell "full" from "not charging" (C14).
  - The NTC value, B and wiring of the -PCM-NTC-LD cell are still unconfirmed (C3, C8, C20).
  - U2, Q1, D1, C3, C4, R1-R5 and R9 still have no MPN (C18).
  - D2 3D model is an envelope from the Bourns SMA maxima (dock/3dmodels/D_SMA_ENVELOPE.step);
    J2 3D model is a datasheet envelope; pin verticality after reflow (bench).
  - Hot-plug VDD on a scope (20 plugs, 1 m and 2 m cables), thresholds, charge termination
    (4.20 V +- 1 %) and fault behaviour are unmeasured (VERIFICATION-PLAN F4).
  - No independent review of these changes; read PCBWay's DFM reply before paying.
