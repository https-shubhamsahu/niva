Blockers closed (9 Nov 2026):
  - J2 = 3 x Mill-Max 0900-0-15-20-76-14-11-0 (same pin as the pod J2), on 2.08 mm round
    pads at 6.0 mm pitch: the datasheet's .082 in minimum pad, as on the C.2 pod.
    Mechanics: the pin is 3.48 mm free, but the Rev C.1 dock put the cartridge pads 3.8 mm
    above the PCB, so it would not have touched. The well floor is now 0.83 mm lower
    (niva_layout DOCK['floor_z'] = PCB top + 2.972 - 1.0 mm cup floor), and
    10_DockBase / 11_DockTop / 12_DockLightPipe_Clear were regenerated. FreeCAD audit
    (mechanical/dock-interference-report.json): 0.50 mm compression seated, 0.40 mm with the
    cartridge lifted onto the latch hook; the only intersection is pins on pads; pins clear
    the well-floor holes by 0.25 mm. Only J2 sits under the well floor (0.77 mm headroom).
  - NTC assumption for R6/R7/R8 (R5 22k pull-up, thresholds 0.570 / 0.180 of VBUS):
    the pack NTC is 10 k at 25 C with B25/50 = 3435 K. Then charging is allowed from
    -0.3 C to 45.1 C nominal. With B 3380 K: -0.7 to 45.5 C. With B 3950 K: 2.7 to 42.3 C.
    A 100 k NTC reads as cold at room temperature and the dock never charges, so a wrong
    NTC shows up on the first test. The cell spec in hand (EEMB LP502030-PCM-LD) has no NTC;
    the NTC value of the -PCM-NTC-LD variant is still unconfirmed.

Passing KiCad ERC/DRC is not a design review. Still open:

    range (NTC tolerance adds to this). Once the purchased cell's NTC is known, retune
    R6/R7/R8 for about 3-42 C. That is a BOM change; the copper does not change.
  - J2 3D model is a datasheet envelope; pin verticality after reflow (bench).
