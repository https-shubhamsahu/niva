Blockers closed from datasheets (9 Nov 2026):
  - F1 = Bourns MF-PSMF035X-2 (0805 PTC: I_hold 0.35 A, I_trip 0.75 A at 23 C,
    6 V, I_max 40 A, R 0.25-1.2 ohm; I_hold 0.30 A at 40 C, 0.27 A at 50 C).
    Cell PCM limits: EEMB LP502030-PCM-LD spec ZJQM-RD-SPC-A03249 6.1: 0.3 A constant
    load, 0.8 A peak for 3 s; cell max discharge 0.5 A, max charge 0.25 A.
    So F1 holds the PCM's continuous rating at 40 C and trips below its 0.8 A peak,
    a second layer inside the PCM envelope. It passes the dock's 100 mA charge.
    The pod's 0.35 A radio bursts last milliseconds; the PTC heats on the average.
    Land: KiCad Fuse_0805_2012Metric (1.0 x 1.45 mm pads); Bourns recommends
    1.0 x 1.5 mm with a 1.2 mm gap. Both cover the terminals; copper left as is.
  - Lead gauge: the same EEMB spec gives UL1571 AWG28 leads, tinned 3.0 +- 0.5 mm,
    20 +- 2 mm long. The 1.5 x 1.1 mm wire pads at 1.8 mm pitch take AWG26-30, so the
    copper suits it (footprint text corrected 9 Nov: pad 1 cell +, pad 2 NTC, pad 3 cell -).

Mechanics that locate this board (9 Nov review, findings D2/C15): end stops in the cup
(03_BatteryCartridgeCup) hold it to 0.15 mm in y, and two posts on the lid hold it down
(0.05 mm gap) against the three dock pins; bond it to the cup floor with epoxy.

Passing KiCad ERC/DRC is not a design review. Still open:
  - F1 series resistance: 0.25 ohm new, up to 1.2 ohm after a trip or reflow, so 88-420 mV
    at a 0.35 A burst. Measure VBAT sag at the pod during radio TX (bench item P1).
    If the pod browns out, MF-PSMF050X-2 (0.15-0.9 ohm, trips by 1.0 A) fits the same
    land but trips above the PCM's 0.8 A peak rating.
  - The EEMB spec read is for LP502030-PCM-LD (two leads, no NTC). The NTC variant the
    design names (-PCM-NTC-LD) needs its own spec: NTC value and third lead gauge.
  - ENIG wear of the dock pads under the Mill-Max plungers: cycle test on the first article
    (consider hard gold or ENEPIG for production, review C16).
  - A tripped F1 dissipates about 0.5 W about 1 mm from the pouch (C5): thermocouple test.
  - No IEC 62133-2 report, UN 38.3 summary or PCM over-current threshold for the cell (C8).
  - No independent review; read PCBWay's DFM reply before paying.
