Passing KiCad ERC/DRC is not a design review. Still open:
  - J2 lateral capture: the 1.067 mm plunger has 0.37 mm side clearance in the 1.8 mm
    plate pocket, and the cartridge can shift about 0.27 mm at the contacts (estimate from
    the 0.4 mm bay clearance and +-0.1 mm at the tab screw). FDM prints pockets undersize:
    measure the first printed lid; a plunger that lands on the pocket rim misses the plate.
  - J2 3D model is a datasheet envelope, not Mill-Max's STEP; check paste volume on the
    2.08 mm pads and pin verticality after reflow (bench).
  - U6: re-read ADI 90-0065 (its code box lists T822-3C, the datasheet T822-3), review
    the EP paste on the stencil, get a supplier 3D model.
  - Antenna: Espressif's 15 mm clearances cannot be met in a 42 mm pod. Range and
    throughput test in the closed pod, on the body, in both roles.
  - The web between the H1 hole and the notch is about 2.4 mm; ask PCBWay to confirm.
  - No independent schematic/layout review and no fabricator DFM report yet;
    read PCBWay's DFM reply before paying.
