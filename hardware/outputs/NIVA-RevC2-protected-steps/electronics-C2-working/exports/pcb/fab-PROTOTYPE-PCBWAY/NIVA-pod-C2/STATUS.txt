Passing KiCad ERC/DRC is not a design review. Still open (Rev C.3 unless noted):
  - 21 open 0.2 mm vias remain in U1 GND pads and EPAD sub-pads: order plugged/filled vias
    or fill and cap before any PCBA (fine for hand-assembled first articles).
  - Power path: the 3.3 V rail can fall below 3.0 V during TX bursts at low cell voltage
    (review A7/C7). Measure first (bench P1).
  - No ESD on the J3 service pads (reachable through the bay window), J1 or the button;
    no 22-33 ohm on USB D+/D- (A22-A24). No pull-ups on CS_ADC/CS_IMU (A2).
  - Analog front end draws 320-540 uA always (A9); MAX17048 CELL is not Kelvin-sensed (B11);
    some bypass caps far from pins and +3V3 is 0.3 mm (B9/B10).
  - J2 3D model is a datasheet envelope; J2 needs hand soldering or a second reflow (B3).
  - U6: re-read ADI 90-0065 (its code box lists T822-3C, the datasheet T822-3).
  - Antenna: Espressif's 15 mm clearances cannot be met in a 42 mm pod; range test.
  - The web between the H1 hole and the notch is about 2.4 mm; ask PCBWay to confirm.
  - No independent review sign-off and no fabricator DFM report yet; read PCBWay's DFM
    reply before paying. No firmware exists for this board yet.
