# SIH 2026: problem statement 26213

**SIH26213:** "Student Innovation: ideas that can boost fitness activities and assist in keeping fit". It is AICTE's MIC Student Innovation statement, in the Hardware category and the Fitness & Sports theme. The idea deadline is **5 October 2026**.

This is one of three Niva projects. See [../PROJECTS.md](../PROJECTS.md).

## The idea

Niva instruments the balance items of the Fit India fitness protocol for ages 18 to 65: the Flamingo test (p. 24) and Vrikshasana (p. 23). Today a tester scores both with a stopwatch and by eye.
- The insole sits on the raised foot and flags events. The tester confirms or dismisses each flag.
- Every trial records how far the insole agreed with the tester.
- Checks F1–F5 then measure that agreement against a trained tester and slow-motion video.

## Rules

- Never invent a number. Every figure needs a source in the same sentence, or it says "to be measured".
- No screening, diagnosis, fall-risk or injury-risk wording.
- No accuracy figures until checks F1–F5 are measured.
- Added hardware, such as a pair of insoles, is allowed.
- The Aavishkar rules in [../aavishkar/AGENTS.md](../aavishkar/AGENTS.md) do not apply here.
