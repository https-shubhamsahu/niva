# NIVA — three footwear concepts

Created using the built-in image-generation tool, referencing the original NIVA hero design. Concept visualization; footwear clearance and attachment require physical fit testing.

## Generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup.
Create a photorealistic industrial design comparison board of NIVA worn in THREE DISTINCT TYPES OF FOOTWEAR. One landscape image, three equal-width vertical photo panels separated by slim white gutters. Simple small navy sans-serif headings at top of each panel, exactly "CHAPPAL", "SANDAL", "SHOE", left to right. No other explanatory captions.
Input reference: existing NIVA worn photograph. Preserve product identity from reference in all three panels: pale grey-blue small rounded rectangular shin pod, navy sidewall, small NIVA wordmark, tiny green status indicator, single circular teal button, tiny R, smooth nonporous navy silicone ladder strap around lower shin with teal keeper. Single slim flat teal sealed flexible connection exits pod bottom, hugs leg and routes along outer ankle to outer heel of insole. Match product scale, shape, colours and materials across all panels. No loose wires, screens or charging ports.
Each panel shows the SAME adult Indian patient's RIGHT lower leg and foot only, no face, natural medium-brown skin, rolled plain taupe trouser hem near top. Crop from below knee to floor, show entire footwear and entire shin pod with space above. Same right-side three-quarter lateral camera angle, toe pointing gently to left, consistent scale and soft daylight, neutral clean concrete floor with subtly blurred primary health centre wall. Photorealistic detailed real skin, realistic weight bearing, footwear and materials, no illustration or cutaway.
LEFT CHAPPAL: simple inexpensive navy rubber flip-flop with one Y-shaped thong upper and toe post between big and second toe. Toes exposed. Thin pale grey-blue NIVA sensing insert lies ON TOP of existing footbed and UNDER foot, visible as thin soft pale rim at toe and heel. Tiny slit in insert accommodates toe post. Discreet pale silicone heel retention loop as reference. Flat teal protected connection originates at lateral rear heel insert and follows ankle to pod. Show very clearly that chappal is a flip-flop not multi-strap sandal.
MIDDLE SANDAL: inexpensive practical brown/dark navy open-toe sandal, TWO wide transverse straps across forefoot and instep and an existing ankle/heel strap, NO toe post. Same thin pale NIVA insert sits ON sandal footbed and UNDER foot, a fine pale edge visible. Existing sandal heel strap retains assembly; do not add duplicate bulky retention straps. Route flat teal tail past outer heel through open side between straps, without pinching at buckle, then up ankle to identical shin pod. This shoe must unmistakably be a strappy open-toe sandal, different from flip-flop.
RIGHT SHOE: plain charcoal inexpensive closed-toe lace-up walking shoe, low collar, simple laces, matte canvas or synthetic upper, modest rubber sole, no brand or athletic graphics. Thin charcoal sock inside shoe ending just above collar. NIVA insert is fully concealed INSIDE shoe UNDER socked foot, do NOT show insole floating outside or on the outsole and do NOT expose toes. Flat teal tail emerges gently between sock and lateral shoe collar near rear, with a short soft grey protective sleeve at collar, lies flush to outer ankle and runs up to identical shin pod on skin. No connector inside shoe or underfoot; no cutting holes in shoe.
Design must read as SAME retrofit NIVA hardware adapted to three ordinary footwear types, not three different products. Keep all feet plausible and grounded. No glowing sensors, exposed sensor disks, underfoot electronics, anatomical x-rays, infographics, arrows, extra limbs or multiple feet per panel. Premium clarity but modest realistic community health equipment.
